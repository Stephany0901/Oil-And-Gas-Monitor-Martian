EQT — Henry Hub Mean-Reversion Hybrid

Long only, two-regime rules-based strategy. Seasonal window (Dec → May): buy last trading day of December, sell last trading day of May. Off-season Z-score (Jun → Nov): enter when HH 20-day M2-aware Z-score < −0.25 and 1-day HH momentum < −$0.20; exit after 10 consecutive signal-free bars. M2-aware lookback: prior-month days in the 20-day window use the second-month HH futures price so the mean/σ window is roll-consistent.
