# Oil & Gas Market Briefing — Thursday, November 12, 2026

*Covers the Thu Nov 12 session. Educational summary, not investment advice.*

## Market

Crude drifted lower on Thursday. In post-market trade Brent was around $91.8 (about −1.1%) and WTI around $85.7. Two things weighed on prices. First, hopes rose for de-escalation on two fronts: US-mediated Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi produced a prisoner exchange, and markets expect no Iran escalation before next week's US–China summit. Second, inventories built more than expected. The EIA report was a day late because of the holiday. It showed commercial crude up **4.66 MMbbl**, against a forecast draw of about 1.1 MMbbl, and gasoline and distillate stocks rose too. The API had flagged a crude build of about 9 MMbbl the night before. Prices did bounce briefly before the US open. Trump posted "We don't need a deal with Iran," and Iran's IRGC claimed that mines disabled two supertankers near Hormuz (a claim not independently verified). Hormuz risk still keeps a floor under Brent, and the Brent–WTI spread is still about $6.

The EIA's November STEO raised its forecasts again. Brent now averages **$97.44 in 2026** and **$85.78 in 2027**, with 4Q26 at about $109. October Brent averaged $124, and the EIA expects about $100 for Nov–Dec. Middle East shut-ins have eased to about 2.5 MMb/d, down from a 10.9 MMb/d peak in May. On supply, OPEC+ output rose in October, led by Saudi Arabia. The core group holds December targets flat and meets next on Nov 29.

US natural gas kept climbing. December NYMEX was about $3.30/MMBtu at midday Thursday, after Wednesday's $3.353 close, the highest since early February, which put the contract up about 12% on the week. Drivers are a cold shot for the East over the weekend, near-record LNG feedgas, softer Lower-48 output (about 112.3 Bcf/d) and short-covering from a record speculative short. Storage is still about 2% above the 5-year average.

Equities ignored the softer crude: XLE +1.5%, XOP +1.7%. Refiners and E&Ps led, while services lagged (XES +0.2%).
