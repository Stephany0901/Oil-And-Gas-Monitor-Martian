# Oil & Gas Briefing — Wednesday, November 4, 2026

## Market
Energy sold off hard on November 4. Crude fell roughly 8–9% (Brent reported below $90/bbl) after the Democratic sweep in the US midterms. Headlines say the result, and Trump and Vance blaming the Iran war for it, raised hopes of de-escalation and an eventual end to the conflict. That removed part of the geopolitical risk premium built into prices since the war began. Exact settlement levels were not confirmed from the sources available to this run, so treat the percentage move as approximate. Prices had already eased on 11/3 on hopes for a diplomatic resolution, despite reports of US strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island.

Equities followed crude down. The sector ETFs fell hard: XLE -4.7%, XOP -6.4%, XES -5.8%. Every priced name closed lower, and the broad market rallied (Dow +1,500 points). Refiners and gas-linked names were hit hardest, so this was a full-sector de-risking, not just a crude-beta move.

Natural gas: no reliable same-day Henry Hub print was found. LNG (Cheniere) fell 6.2% and WMB fell 6.4%, so gas-linked equities sold off with crude. Treat gas direction as unconfirmed.

OPEC+: the group held November output unchanged on October 4 (second straight hold). No new decision surfaced in the past 24 hours. With the war-driven premium fading, watch the next OPEC+ meeting for signs of a policy shift.

Company headlines: Q3 earnings season is in full swing (see below). Marathon Petroleum, Suncor and CVR Energy beat; Ovintiv and SM Energy narrowly missed EPS. Hess (HES) was removed from the watchlist after Chevron closed its acquisition and returns no data.
