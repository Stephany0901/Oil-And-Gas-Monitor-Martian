## Market

Brent rose for a third straight session on Thursday and settled at about **$96.57 (+$1.96, about +2.1%)**, its highest close in several weeks. NYMEX WTI did not settle because of the holiday. It traded near **$84.84 (+1.4%)** in the early afternoon, which leaves the Brent–WTI spread near $11.70. Two Gulf headlines drove the move. Houthi forces claimed an attack on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery on Wednesday. Iran's IRGC then claimed that two tankers hit mines north of Oman's Khasab port, one reportedly carrying more than 2M bbl. No one has independently confirmed the mine claim, and Brent was down about 0.9% in the morning before it rallied. A refinery fire at Bangchak in Thailand and a drone strike on a Russian refinery added to supply worries. All of these figures come from secondary reports of Reuters, so check them against exchange data.

Oversupply still argues for lower prices. The IEA says supply exceeded demand in October for the first time since the Iran war began. Observed inventories rose 52M bbl, and the agency projects 2027 supply of 108.1M b/d against demand of about 104.3M b/d. US crude stocks are at 418.9M bbl after a third straight build. Retail gasoline averages about $3.56/gal, down about 73¢ in a month.

**OPEC+:** Ministers meet Sunday, Nov 29. Sources tell Reuters the group will likely **roll over current targets for at least Q1 2027**, and Rapidan expects the big quota decisions to slip into 2027. Iraq rejects the capacity audit's estimate of under 5M b/d and wants about 6M b/d recognised, against its 4.43M quota. Kazakhstan is also pushing for a higher figure. The UAE is no longer in the group.

**Natural gas:** NYMEX had no settlement on Thursday. January last traded near $3.66/MMBtu after December expired at about $3.27. The first storage withdrawal (24 Bcf, to 3,858 Bcf, about 2% above the five-year average) and record LNG feedgas are offsetting warmer early-December forecasts. Funds hold a large net short (about 236k contracts), which raises the risk of short covering.

**Company headlines:** There was no major US oil and gas M&A or earnings news during the holiday.
