# Oil & Gas Briefing — Thursday, November 19, 2026

## Market

Crude steadied after Wednesday's sell-off but did not bounce. WTI had settled down 3.2% on Wednesday at $83.26, its lowest close since Aug 26, and Brent fell 1.3% to about $91.95. On Thursday both traded roughly flat to slightly firmer: Brent was near $92.0–92.2 and expiring December WTI near $83.3. The bearish pull came from the official EIA report for the week to Nov 13. Commercial crude rose 3.4M bbl to 417.8M against expectations of roughly flat. Gasoline rose 5.2M bbl (about 1.6M expected), distillates rose 1.6M bbl, and total commercial petroleum climbed 9.2M bbl. There were two offsets. Cushing drew 1.2M bbl to 26.1M, and refinery runs stayed high at 95.7% utilization. Geopolitical risk set the floor again. Iranian media reported three tankers disabled by mines east of Hormuz. The Houthis fired missiles at Riyadh after claiming drone hits on East–West pipeline pumping stations, and US–Iran strikes continued around Qeshm, Kuwait and Bahrain. Against that, the IEA expects Gulf exports to recover and sees Q4 supply about 1.7–2 mb/d above demand, so the surplus story still dominates. Brent is on track for a roughly 20% decline in November. One product-market note: deferred refinery maintenance is keeping cracks elevated. PBF's Chalmette/Toledo and Citgo's Lake Charles turnarounds have slipped into 2027, and Valero estimates about 1.5 mb/d of global maintenance has been delayed. The ULSD crack is about $83 and the 3-2-1 crack about $41.5.

**Natural gas:** December Henry Hub settled Wednesday at $3.372 (+5.1%), its highest settle since early February. Thursday's EIA storage report showed a **+20 Bcf injection**, below the 24–25 Bcf consensus, compared with a +15 Bcf five-year average. Working gas is 3,882 Bcf, 80 Bcf (about 2%) above the five-year average and 66 Bcf below last year. Low wind generation raised gas burn. A forecast warm-up through Thanksgiving is the near-term bearish risk. The next storage report is Wed Nov 25, moved up for the holiday.

**OPEC+:** There is no new decision. December quotas are unchanged, and the **Nov 29 ministerial meeting** on 2027 policy and the new capacity-based quota system is the next catalyst.

**Company headlines:** The main corporate news was earnings from small caps and shippers (see below). Exxon's $3.0B notes tender continues. Last week's M&A (FANG–Double Eagle, CNQ–Advantage, reported CVX–Beacon Offshore talks) produced no new developments today.
